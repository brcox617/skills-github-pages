A short, slightly snarky look at five things I think people overhype. This is meant to be opinionated — your mileage may vary.

## 1. Champagne

You can't convince me people aren't pretending to like it because it seems glamorous. It's overly carbonated and has a chemical aftertaste; even expensive bottles can feel more about ritual than flavor.

## 2. Caviar

Try it and disagree — I'm skeptical it lives up to the mystique for most people. The texture and salty blast aren't for everyone.

## 3. Nightclubs

I don't miss shouting to be heard over the music, the cabs, or the lines. For me, the social cost usually outweighs the payoff.

## 4. Picnics

Romantic in theory; in practice it's a hassle — bugs, sand, weather, and carrying everything. A casual outdoor meal is nice, but picnics rarely go exactly as planned.

## 5. Pets

I know this is controversial. Pets are wonderful for many, but they're a big, long-term responsibility people sometimes underestimate. Not saying "don't get one" — just know what you're signing up for.
