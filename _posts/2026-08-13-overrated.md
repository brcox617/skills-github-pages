Top 5 Overrated Things

1. Champagne

  You can't convince me people aren't pretending to like it because it seems glamorous. It's overly carbonated, has a foul aftertaste, and even the top shelf expensive champagne is only worth a small glass. Champagne is a hangover in a bottle.

2. Caviar
  
  Try it and disagree, you can't.

3. Nightclubs
  
  The noise is so loud you can't hear people talk. Tables are absurdly expensive and the waitstaff can't hear you anyway. These were always a regret and I'm thrilled to have aged out of tolerating nightclubs.

4. Picnics
  
  Sit on the ground on a blanket, keep the wildlife from your food, bake in the sun, and carry it all to a more remote location before you eat? No thanks.

5. Pets
  
  This one will anger the 1-2 people that read this, but I'm an adult and I'm fine with you being mad about it. We'll get past it.
